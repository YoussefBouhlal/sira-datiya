# Sira Datiya

A CV presents a person's professional background. Its content is independent of the chosen presentation.

## Language

**CV content**:
The personal details, profile, experience, education, skills, languages, certifications, projects, and references included in a CV.

**Template**:
A visual presentation of CV content. Choosing a different template does not change the content.

**Appearance**:
The chosen template, accent color, text size, and paper size of a CV.

**Portrait**:
An optional image of the person whose CV is being prepared.

**Skill proficiency**:
An optional self-assessed skill level: Beginner, Elementary, Intermediate, Advanced, or Expert. An unrated skill has no proficiency assessment.
_Avoid_: Skill percentage

**Reference**:
A person offered as a professional contact, together with any supplied job title, organization, email, and phone number.
