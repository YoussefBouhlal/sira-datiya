# Sira Datiya

A CV presents a person's professional background. Its content is independent of the chosen presentation.

## Language

**CV content**:
The personal details, profile, experience, education, skills, languages, certifications, projects, and references included in a CV.

**CV section heading**:
The text that labels a section of the CV, distinct from editor navigation labels and the person's professional title.
_Avoid_: Section title

**Custom section heading**:
A person's own wording for a CV section heading, used in the preview and exported CV. It belongs to the CV rather than a particular template and remains as written when the document language changes.

**CV section group**:
Related CV sections presented together under a shared heading, such as languages and certifications under Additional Information. The group's heading is distinct from the headings of its member sections.

**Template**:
A visual presentation of CV content. Choosing a different template does not change the content.

**Template number**:
A permanent, zero-padded catalog label for a template, such as 001. The same label identifies the template in every language and is not reassigned when templates are reordered or retired.
_Avoid_: Template name

**Appearance**:
The chosen template, color scheme, text size, paper size, document language, and proficiency display of a CV.

**CV color scheme**:
The chosen colors for a CV's headings, subtitles and details, body text, accents, and colored surfaces. A color scheme belongs to the CV rather than to an individual template.

**CV color role**:
A named visual purpose within a CV color scheme, such as headings or body text, with a color chosen independently of the other roles.
_Avoid_: Primary color, secondary color, third color

**CV color palette**:
A ready-made combination of colors for the CV color roles, used as a starting point for a color scheme.

**Document language**:
The single language used for the builder interface and default CV labels. It does not determine or translate the person's written CV content or custom section headings.
_Avoid_: Interface language, CV language

**Initial language choice**:
The selection of document language that precedes a person's first template selection.

**CV preview**:
The on-screen representation of the CV with its current content and appearance.

**Template gallery**:
The collection of available templates presented for browsing and comparison.

**Template sample**:
A representation of a template populated with fictional CV content, separate from the person's own CV.

**Template thumbnail**:
A small representation of a template sample.

**ATS-oriented template**:
A template that prioritizes readable CV text for applicant-tracking systems. The label expresses a design goal, not guaranteed compatibility with every system.

**Portrait**:
An optional image of the person whose CV is being prepared.

**Portrait frame**:
The area and shape in a template through which the portrait is visible.

**Portrait framing**:
The chosen position and zoom of the portrait within its frame. The choice belongs to the portrait and remains the same across templates.

**Sample portrait**:
A neutral image placeholder in a template sample that demonstrates where a portrait can appear, separate from the person's own CV.

**Skill proficiency**:
An optional self-assessed skill level: Beginner, Elementary, Intermediate, Advanced, or Expert. An unrated skill has no proficiency assessment.
_Avoid_: Skill percentage

**Proficiency display**:
The presentation of rated skills as a bar, a named level, or both, according to the chosen template's supported presentations. It does not change the skill's proficiency assessment; unrated skills show only their names.
_Avoid_: Skill rating, skill percentage

**Reference**:
A person offered as a professional contact, together with any supplied job title, organization, email, and phone number.
